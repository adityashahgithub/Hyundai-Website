About Hyundai

Driving Innovation Since 1967

Our Story

A journey of innovation and excellence

Founded in 1967 in Seoul, South Korea, Hyundai Motor Company is now among the world’s five largest automakers. Hyundai operates the world’s largest integrated auto plant in Ulsan, produces vehicles in 10+ countries, and sells in over 200 markets. In 2023, Hyundai Motor Company sold over 4 million vehicles globally, driven by strong demand for SUVs and the IONIQ electric lineup.

In India, Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) was established in 1996 with its manufacturing hub near Chennai. The plant’s annual capacity exceeds 800,000 units, serving domestic customers and exporting to 150+ countries. Hyundai crossed 10 million cumulative domestic sales in India and remains a leader in safety, connected tech, and feature-rich SUVs.

Our Journey

1967

Hyundai Motor Company is founded in Seoul, South Korea.

1975

Pony, Korea’s first mass-produced car, debuts and begins exports.

1996

Hyundai Motor India Limited is established; Sriperumbudur plant construction begins.

1998

Santro launches in India, redefining the compact hatchback segment.

2015

Creta launches and becomes a benchmark for compact SUVs in India.

2021

IONIQ 5 debuts on the dedicated E-GMP EV platform, earning global awards.

2024

Hyundai advances its 2045 carbon-neutral roadmap and expands EV and hydrogen programs.
