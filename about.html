About Hyundai

Driving Innovation Since 1967

Our Story

A journey of innovation and excellence

Hyundai Motor Company was founded in 1967 and has since grown to become one of the world's largest automobile manufacturers. With a commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, Hyundai has established itself as a global leader in the automotive industry.

In India, Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) has been a trusted name since 1996, offering a wide range of vehicles that combine style, performance, and advanced technology. We are committed to providing exceptional value to our customers while contributing to a sustainable future.

Our Journey

1967

Hyundai Motor Company was founded in South Korea, marking the beginning of an automotive revolution.

1996

Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) was established, bringing world-class vehicles to Indian roads.

2000

Launched the iconic Santro, which became one of India's most loved hatchbacks.

2015

Introduced the Creta, setting new benchmarks in the compact SUV segment.

2020

Launched the revolutionary IONIQ 5, showcasing Hyundai's commitment to electric mobility.

2024

Continuing to lead with innovation, sustainability, and customer-centric solutions.
